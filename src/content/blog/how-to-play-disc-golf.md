Disc golf is one of the fastest-growing sports in the world, and for good reason — it's free, welcoming, and a genuinely great way to spend a afternoon outdoors. Here's everything you need to know to play your first round.

## The Basic Rules

The concept is simple: throw a disc from a tee pad toward a metal basket, and keep throwing until the disc lands in the basket. Each throw counts as one stroke. Complete the hole in the fewest strokes possible.

**Tee order:** The player with the lowest score on the previous hole tees off first. On hole 1, it's typically a coin flip or random draw.

**Fairway play:** After your tee shot, throw from behind where your previous disc landed. You must maintain a natural stance — one foot behind your lie (the mark where your disc landed).

**Out of bounds:** Some holes have OB lines (paths, roads, water). An OB throw adds one penalty stroke, and you throw again from the previous lie or a designated drop zone.

## Scoring

- **Par** is the expected number of strokes for the hole (usually 3 for most holes)
- **Birdie** = one under par (scored!)
- **Bogey** = one over par (no big deal)
- **Ace** = hole-in-one (the holy grail)

After all 18 holes, total your strokes. Lowest score wins.

## Your First Disc

You don't need a bag full of discs. Start with one midrange disc — it handles both short and medium distances well and is the most forgiving for new players. Popular beginner choices:

- **Innova Mako3** — straight and predictable
- **Discraft Buzzz** — the most popular midrange in the sport
- **Dynamic Discs truth** — reliable with a gentle fade

## Course Etiquette

- **Let faster groups play through** — if someone behind you is waiting, wave them ahead
- **Don't distract throwers** — stay quiet and still when someone is throwing
- **Mark your lie** — place a mini marker disc in front of your thrown disc before picking it up
- **Fill your divots** — if you tear up turf on a tee pad, kick it back in place
- **Pack it out** — leave no trace
