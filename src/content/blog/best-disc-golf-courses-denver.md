Denver might be the best disc golf city in America that doesn't brag about it. The catch: the Mile High City proper has almost no courses — the real scene lives in the suburbs and foothills ringing the metro, where **around 55 courses** sit within a 45-minute drive, per our directory research. Disc golf has been having a moment here lately, but the definitive local guide hasn't been written. This is ours.

A note on honesty before the list: we don't pad this guide with every course on the map. **These are the 4 courses we've actually verified** — real hole-by-hole data, real player ratings, and real addresses — with the rest of the metro on the way. Quality over quantity is the whole point.

**Quick picks:**
- **Best overall:** Beaver Ranch (Conifer) — ★4.8
- **Best free + historic:** Johnny Roberts Memorial (Arvada) — ★4.4
- **The headline:** Bird's Nest (Arvada) — the reported most-played course in the US
- **Best near Red Rocks:** Fehringer Ranch (Littleton) — free, with the metro's best practice area
- **Best mountain round:** Beaver Ranch — seriously, plan a half-day

## 1. Beaver Ranch — Conifer (Best Overall)

Forty-five minutes southwest on US-285, at roughly 8,000 feet in the pine forest above Conifer, sits the course most Colorado disc golfers will tell you is the best in the state. Beaver Ranch is a true mountain course: **21 holes across three tee sets** — the short set plays 4,745 feet at par 63, while the long set stretches past 7,500 feet at par 65. The elevation is constant, the lines are tight, and the awareness that a bad hyzer is disappearing into the trees never leaves you.

It's pay-to-play with camping nearby, and it's the one course on this list worth building a half-day around. Bring discs you're emotionally prepared to lose, and a camera for the [hole-by-hole detail](https://findfrolf.com/co/denver/beaver-ranch).

## 2. Bird's Nest — Arvada (The Headline)

If you only play one course in the Denver metro, this is the honest answer — not because it's the highest rated, but because **Bird's Nest is reported to be the most-played disc golf course in the United States**. Twenty-seven holes on 40 acres of open space, par 82 across nearly 9,500 feet, free, very cart friendly, restrooms on site.

It's a disc-golf-only park with rolling pastures and tree-lined fairways — a workout in the best sense. The [full 27-hole layout](https://findfrolf.com/co/denver/birds-nest) is one continuous loop that never crosses itself. Come on a weekday morning if you want the fairways to yourself.

## 3. Fehringer Ranch — Littleton (Best Near Red Rocks)

The closest verified course to Red Rocks Amphitheatre, and the pick for anyone in town for a show or a hike. Fehringer Ranch is open foothills golf — 18 holes at par 54, free, with rolling grass and hogback views. The wind is the third competitor on exposed days, and the locals will tell you the practice area here is the best in the city.

Sunset rounds with the red rock formations in view are about as Colorado as disc golf gets. [See the course](https://findfrolf.com/co/denver/fehringer-ranch).

## 4. Johnny Roberts Memorial — Arvada (Best Free + Historic)

One of the oldest courses in Colorado — a John Bird design from **1978**, given a "Back to the Future" redesign into the short, technical gem it plays as today. Eighteen holes, par 54, just 3,547 feet — but the mature trees turn every shot into a putter-and-midrange puzzle. It's free, restrooms on site, and a 5-minute drive from Olde Town Arvada for post-round food.

At ★4.4 from nearly 10,000 player reviews, it's the highest-rated free course in our Denver directory — and the best first course to hand to someone who's never thrown a disc. [Full layout here](https://findfrolf.com/co/denver/johnny-roberts).

## The Red Rocks Disc Golf Trip

If you're visiting Denver for a show at Red Rocks Amphitheatre:

- **Before the show:** Fehringer Ranch is 10–15 minutes away — a full round fits if you start early.
- **The upgrade play:** Beaver Ranch is ~35 minutes southwest of Red Rocks. Morning round in the pines, afternoon in Morrison, show at night. That's a perfect Colorado day.
- **Practical notes:** At 5,280 feet, hydrate more than you think you need to, and the sun is stronger than it feels — sunscreen even on overcast days. Summer afternoon lightning is real: if thunder starts, get off the open fairway.

## Season & Altitude Notes

Denver's prime season runs April–October, but Front Range winters are mild enough for year-round play on dry days. Spring is the mud season for shaded courses. The altitude is the thing visitors underestimate: discs fly farther in thin air, which makes the big holes slightly kinder and the short holes sneakier. Play a round and you'll feel the difference by hole 3.
