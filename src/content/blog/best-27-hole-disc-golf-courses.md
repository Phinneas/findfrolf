Some rounds are warm-ups; these are marathons. Every course on this list has **27 or more holes** — a full day of disc golf in one stop. Ranked by player rating.

### 1. [Blue Ribbon Pines](/mn/minneapolis-st-paul/blue-ribbon-pines)

**Blue Ribbon Pines** — East Bethel, Minnesota · 27 holes, par 87, Hard · ★ 4.8 · $8

Hole 18 is the longest at Blue Ribbon at 624 feet — a par 4 that combines everything the course does well: a big drive, a placed second shot, and water close enough to make you think.
