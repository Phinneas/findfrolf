Whether you're new to the sport or sharpening your competitive edge, understanding the official rules of disc golf keeps the game fair and fun. Here's a clear breakdown of the rules that matter most on the course.

## The Object of the Game

Throw a disc from a tee area to a target (metal basket) in as few throws as possible. Complete all holes in the course, and the player with the lowest total stroke count wins.

## Tee Throws

- Players tee off in order of lowest score on the previous hole
- On hole 1, order is determined by draw or mutual agreement
- The tee area is marked — at least one foot must be behind the front edge of the tee pad during your throwing motion
- If your tee shot goes OB, you re-tee with a one-stroke penalty (or use the drop zone if marked)

## Fairway Play

- Your lie is the spot directly behind where your disc came to rest, on the line of play
- You must take a stance with at least one foot on or behind your lie before throwing
- You cannot move your lie to gain an advantage
- If your disc is embedded in the ground (trunk, soft earth), you may mark the disc and lift it, placing it directly behind where it was embedded

## Out of Bounds (OB)

OB areas are marked with white stakes, lines, or natural boundaries (roads, water, fences).

- **Penalty:** One stroke added to your score
- **Next throw:** From the last in-bounds point on the line of play where the disc crossed going out, or from a designated drop zone
- **Provisional throw:** If you're unsure whether a disc is OB, you may throw a provisional to save time, then resolve the ruling later

## Mandatories (Mandos)

A mandatory is a marked obstacle that your disc must pass on a specified side (left or right, or above/below).

- **Penalty for missing:** One stroke, and you re-throw from the tee or designated drop zone
- Mandos are marked with arrows or signage on the course

## Casual Obstacles and Relief

- **Casual relief:** You may take relief without penalty from casual water (puddles), casual obstacles (sticks, trash), or ground under repair
- Mark your lie, pick up the obstacle, and throw from the new position on the line of play behind your lie
- You cannot move permanent natural objects (trees, rocks) that are in your stance or line of play

## Penalties Summary

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| OB throw | +1 stroke, re-throw from last lie or drop zone |
| Missing a mando | +1 stroke, re-throw from tee or drop zone |
| Foot fault (stance violation) | +1 stroke if called by another player |
| Courtesy violation | Warning first, then +1 stroke per occurrence |
| Exceeding time limit | +1 stroke (30 seconds to throw once it's your turn) |

## Putting

- A disc is "in" when it comes to rest in the basket or chains
- A disc resting on top of the basket or wedged in the chains but not in the basket is not in — you must hole out
- You may putt from any stance on or behind your lie within 10 meters (33 feet) of the target
- Beyond 10 meters, a "jump putt" or "step putt" is allowed — you may not putt from a stanced position and follow through past your lie

## Courtesy and Sportsmanship

- Stay quiet and still when another player is throwing
- Do not stand where you might distract the thrower
- If your group is holding up the group behind, invite them to play through
- Help look for lost discs — everyone benefits
