I built this page to answer a simple question. How many courses does a city have for the people who live there? A raw count doesn't tell you much. Chicago has {{stats:city:chicago}} courses, but it also has more than 9 million people.

So I divide our course count by each metro's population and report courses per 100,000 residents. More courses per person usually means more choice close to home. It can also mean less crowding on popular layouts.

The course counts come from the FindFrolf directory. Population comes from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey. A metro here is the full Census metro area, not just the city limits.

This only counts courses in our directory. If a course is missing, the number runs low. Tell me and I'll add it.
