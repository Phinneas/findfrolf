Usage

clear space & care

Clear space & minimum size

Keep padding around the mark equal to the height of the disc. Don't render the emblem below 24px; use the simplified favicon at 16px.

Do & don't

Use the mark on cream, forest, or clay. Keep the wordmark's two-font split: Fraunces "Find," Caveat "Frolf."

✓ On-palette backgrounds✕ Recolor the disc
✓ Generous clear space✕ Stretch or rotate
