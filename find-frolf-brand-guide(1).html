01

What we're building

Find Frolf is a field guide, not a billboard. We map every disc golf course on earth and tell the honest truth about each one — for the weekend beginner and the touring pro alike. Everything in this guide protects that promise.

01

Map everything

Every course, hole by hole, GPS-accurate — drawn by the locals who actually play them each week.

02

Tell the truth

Verified rounds only. No paid placements, no fake reviews, no algorithm deciding what you see.

03

Bring everyone

From a kid's first putter to a 1000-rated drive — the same honest map and a warm welcome to the round.

02

The mark

A field-guide roundel: a basket framed by a topographic ring, with a clay disc caught mid-flight. The wordmark always pairs Fraunces "Find" with a Caveat "Frolf" — structure meeting warmth, which is the whole brand in two words.

Voice & tone

We sound like a knowledgeable local at the first tee: honest, warm, and specific. Never corporate, never hype. If a sentence could appear in any SaaS brochure, rewrite it.

Honest above all

We tell the truth about a course — the mud, the OB road, the missing chains. Trust is the product.

Warm, never corporate

Talk like a person who loves the game, not a platform onboarding a user.

Specific & knowledgeable

"Tight cedar corridors" beats "varied terrain." We actually play; it shows in the details.

Everyone's welcome

Never gatekeep. Beginner and pro get the same respect and the same warm invitation.

07

Motif, icons & motion

The topographic contour line is our connective tissue — it appears faintly behind heroes and dark bands. Icons are simple 2px line work. Motion is subtle and purposeful.

Topographic motif

Faint contour lines tie every page to the outdoors and the field-guide idea. Keep it at 6–8% opacity on light, ~35% on dark.

Icons & motion

Line icons at 2px, rounded caps. Motion is restrained: a live pulse for activity, a gentle lift on hover, the stamped offset shadow.

Photography direction

Real courses and real people — golden-hour light, honest moments, a mix of scenic fairways and players actually playing. Never staged studio shots or generic stock.
